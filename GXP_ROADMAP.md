# GXP roadmap

The concise roadmap is maintained in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). Its sequence is:

1. Stabilise the draft core and add independent validators.
2. Harden identity/security/operations for a controlled pilot.
3. Validate MQTT, interoperability mappings, and real field conditions.
4. Publish measured comparative evidence and gain independent implementations.
5. Prepare public release/governance/standards material only with owner approval.

Global adoption is an objective earned through operating evidence,
interoperability, and governance—not a release label.
